The design. Humans follow waypoint or velocity conditioning (random waypoints here),
detect threats in a configurable collision bubble, switch with a per-step probability
set by their assertiveness and the closest distance in the bubble, keep a committed
intervention (sidestep, veer, pivot or yield), make ordinary direction changes as
gradual or in-place turns, and stop before colliding with a 1 cm target clearance. Two
humans may decide at once; do not add a tie-break. Every rule and output is in
docs/usage/behavior-iteration.md under behavior designs.
