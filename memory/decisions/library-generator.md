## Evidence and what would decide it

ARDY cheaply supplies the current library; Kimodo supplies SOMA geometry. The
[body model](human-body-model.md) decision, turning/facing coverage and visual
quality guide expansion. The [behavior audit](../experiments/2026-10-01-graph-behavior-audit.md)
found abundant sideways/backward travel but almost no slow turning. Coverage and
deliberate facing constraints must be measured before blaming generator identity.
Both generators remain offline only; this question does not reopen that constraint.
