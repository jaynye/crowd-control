update the row it refines) and move its file to the [archive](../../archive/README.md).

| D14 | Prefer body turns then forward walking, tunable rare sidesteps, sticky per-human upright/hunched posture with upright-biased reset, flat-ground vertical feasibility and live Viser stochasticity controls | 2026-10-05, user | Adopted objectives; opt-in implementation, behavior/integration validation incomplete | Load-time annotations preserve the saved graph; new mechanisms default off. | D15 | Simulated humans use full-resolution rigid Core-skin pieces, one per bone, with no simplification, for LiDAR and display. At 4,096 envs on an A40 the plaza step costs 57.3 ms (rigid) against 53.3 ms (capsules) at 16 humans, and the same at 4. There is one body shape; the morphology set and contact geometry stay open in [human body model](../../decisions/human-body-model.md). The `mjlab_plaza` golden pins capsules; no expected file changed |
| D16 | Keep the random-goal plaza design and add an alternate **encounter** design that the iteration agent can also work on. It has waypoint or velocity conditioning, with random waypoints for comparison. A configurable collision bubble feeds a per-step switch probability set by each human's assertiveness and the distance of the closest point in the bubble. Interventions are committed: sidestep, gradual and in-place turns, and yield. Humans stop before colliding, with a starting tolerance of 1 cm. Two humans may decide at once; there is no tie-break. Turning motions of various radii, including in place, should exist and be generated where the library lacks them | 2026-10-06, user | Adopted; encounter design implemented opt-in, turning library in progress | Both designs run through `CrowdController` (`behavior/designs.py`). On development seeds the encounter design has 94% forward travel against 70–83% for plaza, and less joint-sphere body contact. The default graph cannot execute in-place turns. [Encounter design](../../experiments/2026-10-06-encounter-design.md), [turning library](../../experiments/2026-10-06-turning-library.md) |
